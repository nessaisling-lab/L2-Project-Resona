# Resona — naming rationale & trademark homework

## Final name: Resona
From Latin *resonus* ("to resound"). Chosen for sound (distinct, brandable), the
built-in logo hook (the "o" as resonance rings), and fit with the voice/privacy idea.

## Names we evaluated and rejected (don't re-litigate without reason)
- **Cadence** — "CADENCE" is a registered trademark of Cadence Design Systems and
  it covers computer software; cadence.com is theirs; the word is also crowded
  (cycling, music, banks). Highest-risk option for a software product.
- **Kadency / Kadence** — respelling doesn't help: trademark conflict turns on
  *sound*, not spelling, so it's still a Cadence sound-alike. Also collides with
  Kadence WP (WordPress software).
- **Lilt** — beautiful (means the melody of speech) but Lilt is an established
  AI language-translation company. Too close, especially as we touch translation.
- **Scriven** — there's already a Scriven app, and the Scrivener/Scrivo writing-app
  ecosystem owns the "scriv-" space.

## Resona collision landscape (verified)
- **Resona Holdings / Resona Bank** — ~5th-largest Japanese banking group; same
  Latin root; their logo is two R's inside a circle. AVOID an R-in-circle device.
- **Resona Health** — a wellness-device brand (different field).
- No voice/transcription/AI *software* named Resona was found — our lane looks open.
- Trademark is class-based, so a software mark is plausibly registrable vs. a bank
  (financial services), but a famous mark can claim broader protection, search is
  dominated by the bank, and resona.com is taken.

## Pre-launch checklist (do before spending on the brand)
- [ ] USPTO TESS search for "Resona" in the software/Class 9 & 42 space.
- [ ] Secure a distinguishing domain (resona.app / resona.ai / getresona) at a registrar.
- [ ] Check App Store + Play Store name availability.
- [ ] Trademark attorney consult before any commercial launch.
- [ ] Keep the logo clearly different from the bank's R-in-circle (use the ring "o").
- [ ] Glance at competitor slogans (Otter, Whisper) so the tagline stays distinctive.
- [ ] Verify hero headline meets WCAG AA contrast (gold on dark).
- [ ] Confirm the free tier actually honors "no account needed" before homepage claims.
